Transcription calibration

Mark each transcription against its image. Correct: every printed character, symbol and figure is there and right (notation that renders the same, spacing and harmless line breaks are fine; a source typo copied faithfully is correct). Faulty: add each fault with its kind, its severity and where it is. Unsure: can't tell from the image. A fix is optional.
Material: changes what the tagger or a search would take from the question (a wrong or missing concept word, compound, structure, reaction, statement, option's meaning, or figure content the question depends on; added text that brings in another idea). Minor: still reads as the same problem (a wrong digit, exponent, sign, unit, subscript or symbol case, a stray character, spacing, a layout slip that doesn't change the reading). Test: would a tag chosen from this text differ from one chosen from the image?
After you mark a question, the gate's contested positions appear under the panel. Marks save in this browser as you go; Save downloads them as transcription-calibration-marks.json.
