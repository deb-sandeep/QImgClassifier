JEE PHYSICS - TOPIC SCOPE REFERENCE
Each block lists what the topic covers. Match the question to the topic whose
listed sub-topics it actually draws on. topic_name and id are given verbatim -
copy them exactly.

================ GENERAL PHYSICS ================

[id: 102] Units and Measurements
  Units and systems of units, SI units; fundamental and derived units; least
  count, significant figures; errors in measurement (combination of errors);
  dimensions of physical quantities; dimensional analysis and its applications.

[id: 123] Practical Skills
  Experiments and instruments - vernier callipers, screw gauge, metre scale
  and principle of moments; simple pendulum energy dissipation; Young's modulus
  of a wire; surface tension by capillary rise; coefficient of viscosity by
  terminal velocity; speed of sound by resonance tube; specific heat by method
  of mixtures; resistivity / resistance by metre bridge, Ohm's law, post office
  box; galvanometer figure of merit by half-deflection; focal length of mirrors
  and lens by u-v / parallax; angle of deviation vs incidence for a prism;
  refractive index of a glass slab by travelling microscope; p-n junction and
  Zener diode characteristic curves; identification of circuit components.
  Typical: questions about apparatus, measurement procedure, or reading an
  experimental data plot.

================ MECHANICS ================

[id: 103] Kinematics
  Frame of reference; motion in a straight line; speed and velocity; uniform
  and non-uniform motion; average speed and instantaneous velocity; uniformly
  accelerated motion and its equations; velocity-time and position-time graphs;
  relative velocity; motion in a plane; projectile motion; uniform circular
  motion.

[id: 104] Laws of Motion
  Force and inertia; Newton's three laws; momentum and impulse; conservation of
  linear momentum and applications; equilibrium of concurrent forces; static,
  dynamic and rolling friction, laws of friction; dynamics of uniform circular
  motion - centripetal force, vehicle on level and banked roads.

[id: 105] Work, Energy and Power
  Work done by constant and variable forces; kinetic and potential energy;
  work-energy theorem; power; potential energy of a spring; conservation of
  mechanical energy; conservative and non-conservative forces; motion in a
  vertical circle; elastic and inelastic collisions in one and two dimensions.

[id: 106] Rotational Motion
  System of particles and centre of mass; rigid body rotation; moment of a
  force, torque, angular momentum and its conservation; moment of inertia and
  radius of gyration; parallel and perpendicular axes theorems; equilibrium and
  dynamics of rigid bodies with a fixed axis; comparison of linear and
  rotational motion; rolling without slipping.

[id: 107] Gravitation
  Universal law of gravitation; acceleration due to gravity and its variation
  with altitude and depth; Kepler's laws; gravitational potential energy, field
  and potential; escape velocity; satellites - orbital velocity, time period,
  energy, geostationary orbits.

[id: 108] Solids and Liquids
  Elastic behaviour, stress-strain, Hooke's law, Young's / bulk / rigidity
  moduli; fluid pressure, Pascal's law; viscosity, Stokes' law, terminal
  velocity; streamline and turbulent flow, equation of continuity, Bernoulli's
  principle; surface energy and surface tension, angle of contact, excess
  pressure across a curved surface, capillary rise.

[id: 109] Oscillations and Waves
  Periodic motion - period, frequency; simple harmonic motion and its equation,
  phase, energy in SHM; oscillations of a spring; simple pendulum; wave motion,
  longitudinal and transverse waves, speed of a travelling wave, speed of sound
  in gases; progressive wave equation; superposition, reflection of waves,
  standing waves in strings and organ pipes, harmonics; beats; forced and
  damped oscillation, resonance; Doppler effect in sound.

================ OPTICS ================

[id: 110] Ray Optics
  Reflection at plane and spherical mirrors, mirror formula; refraction at
  plane and spherical surfaces; thin lens and lens-maker's formula; total
  internal reflection and applications; magnification, power of a lens,
  combination of lenses and mirrors; refraction and dispersion through a prism;
  microscope and astronomical telescope, magnifying power.

[id: 111] Wave Optics
  Wavefront and Huygens' principle; laws of reflection and refraction by
  Huygens' construction; interference - Young's double-slit experiment, fringe
  width, coherent sources; diffraction at a single slit, width of central
  maximum; polarization - plane-polarized light, Brewster's law, Polaroid.

================ THERMAL PHYSICS ================

[id: 112] Heat
  Heat and temperature; thermal expansion of solids, liquids and gases;
  specific heat capacity, calorimetry; change of state, latent heat; heat
  transfer - conduction, convection, radiation; Newton's law of cooling;
  blackbody radiation, Kirchhoff's law, Wien's displacement law, Stefan's law.

[id: 113] Thermodynamics
  Thermal equilibrium and temperature, zeroth law; heat, work and internal
  energy; first law of thermodynamics; isothermal and adiabatic processes;
  second law of thermodynamics; reversible and irreversible processes; Carnot
  engine and its efficiency.

[id: 114] Kinetic Theory of Gases
  Equation of state of a perfect gas; work done in compressing a gas;
  assumptions of kinetic theory; kinetic interpretation of pressure and
  temperature; RMS speed; degrees of freedom; specific heats Cv and Cp for
  monatomic and diatomic gases; law of equipartition of energy; mean free path;
  Avogadro's number.

================ ELECTROMAGNETISM ================

[id: 115] Electrostatics
  Electric charge and conservation; Coulomb's law, superposition; electric
  field and field lines; electric dipole and torque on it; electric flux,
  Gauss's law and applications (wire, sheet, shell); electric potential and
  potential difference, equipotential surfaces; conductors and dielectrics,
  polarization; capacitors and capacitance, combinations, parallel-plate
  capacitor with / without dielectric, energy stored.

[id: 116] Current Electricity
  Electric current, drift velocity, mobility; Ohm's law, resistance,
  resistivity and conductivity; I-V characteristics of ohmic and non-ohmic
  conductors; electrical energy and power; series and parallel combinations of
  resistors; temperature dependence of resistance; internal resistance, EMF and
  terminal potential difference, combination of cells; Kirchhoff's laws;
  Wheatstone bridge and metre bridge.

[id: 117] Magnetism
  Biot-Savart law and circular loop; Ampere's law - straight wire and solenoid;
  force on a moving charge and on a current-carrying conductor; force between
  parallel currents; torque on a current loop; moving-coil galvanometer and its
  conversion; current loop and bar magnet as magnetic dipoles; magnetic field
  of a dipole; para-, dia- and ferromagnetism, effect of temperature.

[id: 118] EM Induction and AC
  Electromagnetic induction - Faraday's law, induced EMF and current, Lenz's
  law, eddy currents; self and mutual inductance; alternating current - peak and
  RMS values; reactance and impedance; LCR series circuit, RC / LR / LC / LCR
  circuits with dc and ac sources; resonance, power in ac circuits, wattless
  current; ac generator and transformer.

[id: 119] EM Waves
  Displacement current; electromagnetic waves and their characteristics,
  transverse nature; electromagnetic spectrum - radio waves, microwaves,
  infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-rays, gamma rays; applications of
  electromagnetic waves.

================ MODERN PHYSICS ================

[id: 120] Matter and Radiation
  Dual nature of radiation; photoelectric effect - Hertz and Lenard's
  observations, Einstein's photoelectric equation, particle nature of light;
  matter waves and the de Broglie relation; characteristic and continuous
  X-rays, Moseley's law.

[id: 121] Atoms and Nuclei
  Alpha-particle scattering, Rutherford's model; Bohr model, energy levels,
  hydrogen spectrum; composition and size of the nucleus, atomic masses;
  mass-energy relation, mass defect, binding energy per nucleon; nuclear
  fission and fusion; alpha, beta and gamma radiation; law of radioactive
  decay, decay constant, half-life and mean life.

[id: 122] Electronic Devices
  Semiconductors; semiconductor diode - I-V characteristics in forward and
  reverse bias; diode as a rectifier; LED, photodiode, solar cell; Zener diode
  and its use as a voltage regulator; logic gates (OR, AND, NOT, NAND, NOR).

================ FOUNDATION ================

[id: 124] Foundation Mathematics
  Pre-requisite mathematical tools used in solving physics problems - algebra,
  basic calculus (differentiation and integration of standard functions),
  quadratic and simultaneous equations, graphs, approximations and series
  expansions. Use only when a question is purely about the mathematical
  technique rather than a physics concept.

[id: 125] Foundation Vectors
  Pre-requisite vector tools - vector addition and resolution, components, unit
  vectors, dot and cross products and their geometrical meaning, as applied to
  physics problems. Use only when a question is purely about vector manipulation
  rather than a physics concept.
