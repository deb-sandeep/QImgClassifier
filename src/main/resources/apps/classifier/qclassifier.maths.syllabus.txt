JEE MATHS - TOPIC SCOPE REFERENCE
Each block lists what the topic covers. Match the question to the topic whose
listed sub-topics it actually draws on. topic_name and id are given verbatim -
copy them exactly.

================ SET THEORY ================

[id: 79] Sets
  Sets and their representation; kinds of sets (empty, finite, infinite); power
  set; union, intersection, complement, difference and symmetric difference and
  their algebraic properties; De Morgan's laws and problems based on them;
  Cartesian product of finite sets.

================ ALGEBRA ================

[id: 80] Relations and Functions
  Relations, types of relations, equivalence relations; domain, codomain,
  range; function as a special relation; one-one, into and onto functions;
  invertible functions; even and odd functions; standard functions
  (polynomial, trigonometric, exponential, logarithmic, power, absolute value,
  greatest integer); sum, difference, product and composition of functions;
  graphs of simple functions.

[id: 81] Complex Numbers
  Complex numbers as ordered pairs, a + ib form; Argand diagram; modulus and
  argument; algebra of complex numbers, conjugation; polar representation;
  properties of modulus and principal argument; triangle inequality; cube roots
  of unity; geometric interpretation of complex-number operations.

[id: 82] Theory Of Equations
  Quadratic equations in real and complex systems and their solutions;
  relations between roots and coefficients; nature of roots; formation of
  equations with given roots; fundamental theorem of algebra; symmetric
  functions of roots; higher-degree polynomial equations; location of roots.

[id: 83] Sequence and Series
  Arithmetic and geometric progressions; insertion of AM and GM between two
  numbers; relation between AM and GM; sums of finite AP and GP; infinite
  geometric series; sum of the first n natural numbers, and of their squares
  and cubes.

[id: 84] Logarithms
  Logarithms and their properties; logarithmic equations and inequalities.

[id: 85] Permutations and Combinations
  Fundamental principle of counting; permutations and combinations; meaning of
  P(n,r) and C(n,r); simple applications of permutation and combination.

[id: 86] Binomial Theorem
  Binomial theorem for a positive integral index; general term and middle term;
  properties of binomial coefficients and simple applications.

[id: 87] Matrices
  Matrices of order two and three; algebra of matrices (addition, product);
  types of matrices; equality of matrices; transpose; elementary row and column
  transformations; diagonal, symmetric and skew-symmetric matrices.

[id: 88] Determinants
  Determinants of a square matrix of order up to three; evaluation of
  determinants; area of a triangle using determinants; adjoint and inverse of a
  matrix; test of consistency; solution of simultaneous linear equations in two
  or three variables.

[id: 89] Probability
  Random experiment, sample space, types of events; probability of an event;
  addition and multiplication theorems; independence of events; conditional
  probability, total probability and Bayes' theorem; probability distribution,
  mean and variance of a random variable; use of permutations and combinations
  in probability.

[id: 90] Statistics
  Measures of central tendency and dispersion; mean, median and mode of grouped
  and ungrouped data; standard deviation, variance and mean deviation;
  comparison of frequency distributions with the same mean but different
  variance.

[id: 91] Vector Algebra
  Vectors and scalars; addition of vectors; components of a vector in two and
  three dimensions; scalar (dot), vector (cross) and scalar triple products;
  geometrical interpretation of vector products.

================ TRIGONOMETRY ================

[id: 92] Trigonometry
  Trigonometric identities and functions, their periodicity and graphs;
  addition and subtraction formulae; multiple- and sub-multiple-angle formulae;
  general solution of trigonometric equations; inverse trigonometric functions
  (principal value) and their elementary properties; heights and distances.

================ GEOMETRY ================

[id: 93] Coordinate System
  Cartesian coordinates in a plane; distance and section formulae; locus and
  its equation; slope of a line; parallel and perpendicular lines; intercepts
  on the axes; shift of origin.

[id: 94] Straight Line
  Various forms of the equation of a line; intersection of lines; angle between
  two lines; concurrence of three lines; distance of a point from a line; line
  through the intersection of two given lines; angle-bisector equations;
  centroid, orthocentre, incentre and circumcentre of a triangle.

[id: 95] Circles
  Standard and general form of the equation of a circle, its centre and radius;
  equation from the endpoints of a diameter; equations of tangent, normal and
  chord; parametric equations; intersection of a circle with a line or another
  circle; family of circles through intersection points.

[id: 96] Conic Sections
  Equations of a parabola, ellipse and hyperbola in standard form; foci,
  directrices and eccentricity; parametric equations; equations of tangent and
  normal.

[id: 97] 3D Geometry
  Coordinates of a point in space; distance and section formulae; direction
  ratios and direction cosines; angle between two lines; equation of a line;
  skew lines and the shortest distance between them; distance of a point from a
  plane; angle between two planes and between a line and a plane; coplanar
  lines.

================ CALCULUS ================

[id: 98] Limit and Continuity
  Limit of a function at a real number; continuity and differentiability;
  limits and continuity of sum, difference, product and quotient of functions;
  L'Hopital's rule; continuity of composite functions; intermediate value
  property.

[id: 99] Derivatives
  Derivative of a function; differentiation of sum, difference, product and
  quotient; chain rule; derivatives of polynomial, rational, trigonometric,
  inverse trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic functions; higher-order
  and implicit differentiation; rate of change; increasing and decreasing
  functions; maxima and minima of one variable; tangents and normals; Rolle's
  theorem and Lagrange's mean value theorem and their geometric interpretation.

[id: 100] Integral Calculus
  Integral as an anti-derivative; fundamental integrals (algebraic,
  trigonometric, exponential, logarithmic); integration by substitution, by
  parts and by partial fractions; integration using trigonometric identities;
  fundamental theorem of calculus; properties and evaluation of definite
  integrals; area of regions bounded by simple curves.

[id: 101] Differential Equations
  Ordinary differential equations; order and degree; solution by separation of
  variables; solution of homogeneous and linear first-order differential
  equations.
